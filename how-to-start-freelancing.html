How to Start Freelancing

Step 1: Learn One Skill

Choose one skill such as graphic design, web development, writing or digital marketing.

Step 2: Practice Daily

Create sample projects and improve your portfolio.

Step 3: Create Profiles

Open accounts on Fiverr and Upwork with a professional profile.

Step 4: Publish Your Portfolio

Show your best work and keep improving it.

Step 5: Keep Learning

Success in freelancing comes with consistency and continuous learning.
